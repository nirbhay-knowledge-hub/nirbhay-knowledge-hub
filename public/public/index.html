Safety Knowledge

HIRA

Hazard Identification & Risk Assessment: A proactive methodology to identify workplace hazards, evaluate associated risks, and implement robust controls to eliminate incidents before they occur.

PTW

Permit to Work: A formal, documented safety system designed to safeguard high-risk operations (such as confined space entry or hot work) under strictly authorized conditions.

Risk Assessment

A systematic process of evaluating risks arising from hazards, analyzing existing control measures, and prioritizing actions to reduce vulnerability to acceptable levels.
